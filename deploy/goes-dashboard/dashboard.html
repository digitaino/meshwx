Mesh weather bot · WX-AUS

The dish receives the GOES-19 HRIT downlink. NOAA relays EMWIN inside it: every National Weather Service text product for the whole country. The bot parses those products as they arrive and answers over LoRa on the MeshCore mesh. No internet connection is involved. A reply is one mesh packet.

To use it on the Austin mesh

  1. Radio: US MeshCore default (910.525 MHz, 62.5 kHz, SF7, CR5).
  2. Add the channel #meshwx in the MeshCore app. Type the name only; the key is derived from it.
  3. Post a command, for example wx round rock tx. Replies go by DM when the bot knows your node.
  4. Or DM WX-AUS directly. Its advert has to be in your contacts first. It adverts every few hours and whenever a stranger talks to it on the channel.
  5. Products cover the whole country. If another WX- bot is nearer to the place you ask about, that bot answers.
