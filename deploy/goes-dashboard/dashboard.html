Use the bot

The dish receives NOAA's EMWIN broadcast from GOES-19: every National Weather Service text product for the whole country. The bot reads them as they arrive and answers on the MeshCore mesh over LoRa. A reply is one mesh packet, and normally no internet is involved.

  1. Set your radio to the US MeshCore default (910.525 MHz, 62.5 kHz, SF7, CR5).
  2. Add the channel #meshwx in the MeshCore app. Type the name only; the key comes from it.
  3. Post a command there, for example wx round rock tx. Replies go by DM when the bot knows your node.
  4. Or DM WX-AUS directly once its advert is in your contacts. It adverts every few hours and whenever someone new talks to it.
  5. Products cover the whole country. If another WX- bot is nearer to the place you ask about, that bot answers.

The MeshWX iOS app reads the bot's binary weather messages on #meshwx.

Radar and charts

Radar mosaics and most charts come down with EMWIN, so they run as late as the feed does; the surface analysis and the tropical Atlantic charts come with HRIT's NWS channel. A loop plays its frames in the order they were made, whatever order they reached the dish.

EMWIN feed

EMWIN is the weather-text part of the downlink (channels 20 to 22): every National Weather Service product, sent up by NOAA. When NOAA's uplink falls behind, everything here runs late, however clean the signal.
