Console

Channels

Warnings, observations, forecasts as compact datagrams
Apps ping here to find nearby bots
Applied on the node immediately: the role's slot is renamed in place, or a slot that already has the name is reused. Never uses slot 0. Renaming a tagged slot on the Radio page moves the role with it.
